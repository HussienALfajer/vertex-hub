# F23 — Departments management

Status: Approved · Date: 2026-10-28 · Scope: `docs/product/v2-scope.md` §F23 · ADRs: 0013, 0014, 0016, 0017, 0018, 0037, 0038, 0039, 0043

## Summary
The ten departments are fixed in code today: the General Manager can rename one and change its manager, but cannot open a new department, close one the agency no longer runs, or manage its people from its own page, and a department's page says nothing about its work. F23 turns departments into records the General Manager creates and archives, with a wizard that moves a department's people and open work to other departments before it closes. Each department gets an identity (description, color, icon, order) shown across the Hub. Its page manages members, shows what the department may do, what it is working on, and its history. Members and managers hear about membership changes.

## In scope / out of scope
- In (the first package the owner approved on 2026-10-28, items 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12, 13 and 16):
  - Creating a department (ADR 0043: the enum columns become text codes referencing `departments`).
  - Archiving a department through a transfer wizard, and restoring it. No department is protected (owner); a system department's function falls to the General Manager while it is archived (owner).
  - Identity: description, color from a fixed palette, icon from a curated set, and the order of departments; color and icon shown everywhere a department appears (owner).
  - Members managed from the department page: add, make primary, move, remove.
  - What a department grants: its member and manager permissions and its system functions, read-only.
  - The department overview on its page: counts for everyone, workload and absences for its managers (owner).
  - The department's history tab, from the audit log.
  - Notices to the department manager and the member when a member joins or leaves (owner).
- Out (later or never):
  - The second package, approved in principle (owner, 2026-10-28), each in its own spec: a deputy manager who acts during the manager's leave (item 5), system functions moved between departments (item 8), and intake settings for department requests (item 9).
  - Editing a department's capabilities from the UI (item 7), a brief-fields builder per department (item 10), department goals (item 15): deferred.
  - Hard deletion of a department (archive, don't delete). Merging two departments in one step (archive one with all categories moved to the other does it). Sub-departments.
  - Brief fields (F06B rule C7) and capabilities for new departments: a new department has none.

## Roles and access
New permission `departments.manage`: General Manager only (through `everything`). Nobody else holds it.

## Data

### `departments` (owned by `auth`; existing table, changed)
| Field | Type | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| `code` | text | required, unique; was enum `department_code`. The ten seeded codes stay; a new department gets `d_` + 8 lowercase base-32 characters, generated by the API, never shown or changed (ADR 0043) |
| `name` | text | required, 1–60 characters, unique regardless of case across active and archived departments (F01 rule 21) |
| `description` | text | new, nullable, at most 500 characters, plain text |
| `color` | enum `department_color` | new, required: `c1` … `c8`; the ten seeded departments get `c1`…`c8`, `c1`, `c2` in seed order |
| `icon` | text | new, required, one of `DEPARTMENT_ICONS` (contracts); the seeded departments get a fitting icon each (migration) |
| `position` | integer | new, required; the order of active departments; seeded in id order (1–10); a new or restored department goes last |
| `manager_id` | uuid → `users.id` | unchanged |
| `archived_at` | timestamptz | now used: set by archiving, cleared by restoring. An archived department has no members and no manager |

### Department columns on other tables (ADR 0043)
Each becomes `text` holding a department code with a foreign key to `departments.code`, values unchanged:
`tasks.department`, `task_comments.addressed_department`, `work_template_steps.department`, `work_template_assignees.department`, `catalog_services.department`, `quote_lines.department`, `quote_line_items.department`. The arrays `projects.departments` and `retainers.departments` become `text[]` (GIN index kept); their services check every code exists. The enum `department_code` is dropped. One migration, reviewed with `/db-migration`; it changes types in place, so it is backward-compatible for reads.

### Contracts (`packages/contracts`)
- `departmentCodeSchema`: a string matching `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{1,39}$`. `SYSTEM_DEPARTMENT_CODES`: the ten seeded codes (today's `DEPARTMENT_CODES`, renamed); `DEPARTMENT_CAPABILITIES` and `DEPARTMENT_BRIEF_KINDS` are keyed by them.
- `DEPARTMENT_COLORS` (`c1`…`c8`) and `DEPARTMENT_ICONS` (about 30 Lucide names fitting agency work: megaphone, palette, camera, code, pen-tool, film, stethoscope, briefcase, headset, chart-line, users, building, …).
- `DEPARTMENT_SYSTEM_FUNCTIONS`: per system code, the functions the code gives it, as keys the screens translate (rule 30).
- `departmentResponseSchema` gains `description`, `color`, `icon`, `position`, `archivedAt`.
- Department colors are design tokens `--department-1` … `--department-8` in `packages/ui` (light and dark), checked for contrast like the other tokens.

`department` (existing). New actions listed under "Audit, notifications and jobs".

## States and rules

### Departments
`active` (archived_at null) ⇄ `archived`. Archive needs a transfer plan; restore needs nothing.

1. Only `departments.manage` creates, archives and restores. Names, identity, order, manager and members need `users.manage`, on active departments only (`DEPARTMENT_ARCHIVED` on an archived one).
2. Creating takes a name, an optional description, a color and an icon; the department starts with no members and no manager and goes last in the order. Its code is generated (rule ADR 0043) and appears nowhere in the UI.
3. Names are unique regardless of case across active and archived departments (`DEPARTMENT_NAME_TAKEN`): restoring never collides.
4. The order is one list of active departments; reordering sends the whole list of active ids (`DEPARTMENT_ORDER_MISMATCH` when it is not exactly the active set). Every list, picker and filter of departments uses this order; archived departments come after active ones where they appear.
5. Pickers (new task, request to a department, department question, catalog service, template step and default assignee, project and retainer departments, announcement audience, user form) list active departments only. Choosing an archived department is refused (`DEPARTMENT_ARCHIVED`).
6. Filters (tasks, board, workload, projects, retainers, catalog, productivity report, team) list active departments; a filter value naming an archived department still works and shows its name with an "archived" mark.
7. A record that keeps an archived department (a closed task, a closed project, an accepted quote's lines, a historical question) shows its name and color with an "archived" mark.

### Archiving and the transfer
8. The archive wizard first shows what the department holds, by category, from `GET /api/departments/:id/archive-preview`:
   - **members**: primary members, secondary members, and the manager;
   - **tasks**: open tasks (`OPEN_TASK_STATUSES`), assigned and in the department's queue;
   - **questions**: open questions addressed to the department;
   - **services**: catalog services it performs (active and archived) and the lines of open quotes (`OPEN_QUOTE_STATUSES`) and their items naming it;
   - **templates**: template steps and default-assignee rows naming it, on active templates;
   - **engagements**: open projects (`OPEN_PROJECT_STATUSES`) and active or paused retainers listing it among their departments.
9. The General Manager picks one target department (active, not this one) and may change the target of any category (owner). A category with nothing in it needs no target. Archiving is refused if any non-empty category has no target (`TRANSFER_TARGET_REQUIRED`) or a target is archived or the department itself (`INVALID_TRANSFER_TARGET`).
10. Everything moves in one transaction, together with setting `archived_at`:
    - the manager is cleared (they stop being its manager; rule 2 of ADR 0014 recomputes their roles);
    - primary members become primary members of the members target; if they were already secondary there, that row becomes primary. Secondary memberships in the archived department are removed;
    - open tasks change department to the tasks target and keep their assignee, status, dates and history; tasks in the queue land in the target's queue;
    - open questions addressed to the department are readdressed to the questions target; a question already forwarded to a member keeps that member;
    - services and open quote lines and items change department to the services target;
    - template steps change department to the templates target; a default-assignee row moves to the target when the template has none for it and the person is a member of the target after the move, otherwise it is removed (steps then go to the target's queue, as F07 does);
    - open projects and active or paused retainers replace the department with the engagements target in their list (no duplicate when the target is already listed).
11. Closed and historical records keep the archived department: done and cancelled tasks, answered questions, closed projects, ended retainers, and quotes that are accepted, rejected or superseded.
12. The preview is advisory: archiving recomputes every category inside its transaction (`lockAccessChanges`), so work created between the preview and the confirmation moves too.
13. An archived department grants no capabilities (it has no members) and appears in no audience resolution, escalation target or notice recipient list.

### System functions while archived (owner)
14. While a system department is archived, every notice, reminder or escalation step addressed to its members or managers goes to the General Managers instead. This covers: the Internal Operations manager (escalations, invoice and retainer follow-ups, question and over-limit reminders, HR reminders, new-client notices), Medical Consultation members (medical review requests), the sales departments (General Communication and Marketing lead reminders and lead owners), and the escalation chain targets naming Content Management or Photography. Where the original list also contains other people, the General Managers replace only the archived department's share. The General Manager holds every permission, so every such action stays possible.
15. The archive dialog lists the functions of a system department (from `DEPARTMENT_SYSTEM_FUNCTIONS`) and says they move to the General Manager until it is restored.

### Restoring
16. Restoring clears `archived_at` and places the department last in the order. It comes back with no members and no manager; nothing moved by the archive moves back. A system department's function returns to it at once (its notices go to its members again, once it has some).

### Members (from the department page)
17. Adding takes up to 20 non-archived users who are not members yet; they join as secondary members. Invited users can be added (F01 lets a user be a member before activation).
18. "Make primary here" makes this department the member's primary one; their previous primary department stays as a secondary membership (owner).
19. "Move to another department" moves this membership to the chosen active department, keeping its kind (primary stays primary, secondary stays secondary), and removes it here. When the member already belongs to the target, the memberships merge: primary wins.
20. "Remove" removes a secondary membership only. A primary membership is moved or changed, never removed (`PRIMARY_DEPARTMENT_REQUIRED`, F01 edge case 6).
21. A manager's membership can be neither moved nor removed until the manager changes (`MANAGER_MEMBERSHIP_REQUIRED`, F01 rule 8).
22. Archived users cannot be changed (`USER_ARCHIVED`). Every rule of the F01 user form applies, and the user form keeps editing departments the same way.
23. A membership change applies to permissions at once (ADR 0014, F01 rule 4).

### Overview
24. Counts for everyone, for the department's code: open tasks by status, overdue open tasks, open tasks due this week (Saturday to Friday, as the F06 workload), tasks in the queue without an assignee, catalog services it performs (not archived), open projects and active or paused retainers that list it.
25. For `reports.read` covering the department (rule table): per active member, open tasks, overdue and due this week (F06 workload rules), and who is on approved leave today (name and dates only, never the leave type, as on the company calendar, F17).
26. An archived department shows no overview; its page shows the identity, the archive date and who archived it, and a "Restore" action for `departments.manage`.

### What a department grants
27. The page lists the permissions a member and the manager receive from `DEPARTMENT_CAPABILITIES`, each as a translated sentence, and the department's system functions (rule 30). A department with neither says so.
28. Every permission gets an Arabic label (`permissions.<name>` in the web locale), used here.

### History
29. The tab lists audit entries with `entityType = department` and this id, newest first, paginated as the audit log (F01). It shows creation, every edit, manager changes, membership changes, archive with its counts, and restore.

### System functions (rule 15, rule 27)
30. `DEPARTMENT_SYSTEM_FUNCTIONS` names, per system code: `internal_operations` → escalations and follow-ups, user and system management; `medical_consultation` → medical review; `general_communication`, `marketing` → lead ownership and reminders; `content_management` → content management and review; `photography` → shoots. General Management, Public Relations, Design and Development have none.

Every endpoint that accepts a department code (tasks, questions, catalog, templates, projects, retainers, audiences, filters) validates it against the departments table: an unknown code is `UNKNOWN_DEPARTMENT`, an archived one on a write is `DEPARTMENT_ARCHIVED`; filters accept archived codes.

## Screens

### Departments `/departments`
- Cards in the department order, each with its color stripe and icon, name, description (two lines), capability line, manager and member count.
- `users.manage`: a "Reorder" mode (drag handles, and up/down buttons for keyboard and touch; Save or Cancel) and an "Archived" toggle showing archived cards muted with their archive date.
- `departments.manage`: "New department" opens a dialog: name, description, color swatches, icon grid; on save, the new department's page opens.
- Loading: card skeletons. Empty archived view: "No archived departments". Error: retry.

### Department `/departments/$departmentId`
- Header: icon on the department color, name, description, archived badge if archived; actions by permission: "Edit" (name, description, color, icon), "Change manager", "Archive" (`departments.manage`), "Restore" (archived, `departments.manage`).
- Tabs: **Overview** (default), **Members**, **What it grants**, **History** (only with `audit.read`).
- **Overview**: count tiles (open by status, overdue, due this week, in the queue, services, projects and retainers), each linking to the filtered list; for rule 25, a workload table per member and an "On leave today" list. Empty department: an empty state that invites adding members.
- **Members**: the manager card and the members list (as today) plus, for `users.manage`: "Add members" (a multi-select of users who are not members, with search by name), and per member a menu: "Make primary here", "Move to another department…" (a department picker), "Remove" (secondary only). Refusals show their reason inline (manager, primary).
- **What it grants**: two lists (members, manager) of translated permissions, and the system functions; or "This department grants no extra permissions".
- **History**: the audit entries as the audit page renders them, paginated.
- Archived: identity, archive date and actor, members tab empty, history; no overview.

### Archive wizard (dialog, two steps)
1. **What moves**: one row per non-empty category with its count and a short list (first five), a target picker per row prefilled from a "Move everything to" picker at the top; the system-function warning (rule 15) when it applies.
2. **Confirm**: a summary sentence per category ("3 open tasks move to Design"), and "Archive" (destructive style). On success the archived page shows, with a toast.
- Refusals (a target archived meanwhile) return to step 1 with the reason.

### Department identity across the app (owner)
`DepartmentChips`, task cards and rows, the board's department filter, project and retainer department lists, the catalog and the workload screen show the department's color dot and icon beside its name, through one `DepartmentBadge` component in `apps/web` built on `packages/ui` tokens. Archived departments show muted with the archived mark.

All screens RTL, logical CSS, i18next strings, design-system components only.

## Audit, notifications and jobs

### Audit (entity `department`)
- `department.created` (name, description, color, icon), `department.updated` (changed fields, as today), `department.reordered` (one entry on each moved department: old and new position).
- `department.member_added`, `department.member_removed`, `department.primary_changed` (the person and the kind), written on the department for every membership change, from the department page, the user form and the archive transfer. The user's own `user.updated` entry stays as in F01.
- `department.archived` (the targets and the counts moved per category) and `department.restored`.
- Moved records write their own module's audit entry where that module audits a department change (tasks: `task.updated` with the department; projects and retainers: their departments; services: `catalog_service.updated`), with the archive named as the reason.

### Notifications (F16 catalog, category `account`, priority info, never to the actor)
- `department_member_added`: to the member ("You joined X as primary/secondary") and to the department's manager ("Y joined X"). Opens the department.
- `department_member_removed`: to the member and to the department's manager. Opens the department.
- `department_archived`: to each member moved by an archive ("X was archived; you moved to Z") and to each target department's manager, once, with the counts it received. Opens the target department.
- The existing `department_manager_assigned` and `responsibility_moved` stay as they are.
- Email and push follow the channel policy of the info category (ADR 0037); the morning digest is unchanged.

### Jobs
None. Archiving runs in the request; at about 20 users and 20 clients it moves at most a few hundred rows.

## Edge cases
1. Two General Managers archive the same department at once: the second gets `DEPARTMENT_ARCHIVED`.
2. A department is archived while someone has a new-task form open with it selected: the save is refused with `DEPARTMENT_ARCHIVED`, and the form reloads the departments.
3. The members target is archived between the preview and the confirmation: `INVALID_TRANSFER_TARGET`, nothing moves.
4. Archiving a department whose manager is also its only member: the manager is cleared and moves as a member to the target; they stop being a department manager unless they manage another department.
5. Archiving the department of every General Manager (General Management): they move like everyone else; nothing about the General Manager role depends on the department.
6. A user's only department is archived: they always move (primary members always move), so every active user keeps exactly one primary department (F01 rule 1).
7. A department has an open template run whose next steps name it: the steps were moved with the templates category; tasks already generated are tasks and moved with the tasks category.
8. A retainer cycle opens after the archive: it reads its template steps, now on the target.
9. A stored announcement audience names an archived department: it resolves to nobody for that part (rule 13); the audience editor shows it with the archived mark and lets the author remove it.
10. A URL filter or a saved link names an archived department: the filter shows it (rule 6); its page opens as archived.
11. Restoring a department whose name another department took meanwhile cannot happen (rule 3).
12. Reorder with a stale list (someone created or archived a department meanwhile): `DEPARTMENT_ORDER_MISMATCH`, the screen reloads the list.
13. Adding a user who became archived meanwhile: `USER_ARCHIVED` naming them; nobody is added (all or nothing).
14. "Make primary here" for a member who manages their old primary department: allowed; they stay its manager, as a secondary member (F01 rule 8 accepts secondary members as managers).
15. Moving or removing the actor's own membership: allowed under the same rules (the Operations manager cannot move out of Internal Operations while managing it, rule 21); any permission it changes applies from the next request.
16. The overview of a department with no members: counts can still be non-zero (queue tasks); the workload table is empty.
17. Department question escalation to an archived department after it was archived: never happens, open questions moved; an answered question reopened later is addressed to its (archived) department and is readdressed to the General Managers by rule 14 until someone forwards it.
18. A seeded department's code appears in code (capabilities, brief kinds, recipients): it never changes, archived or not, so restoring restores all of it.

## Acceptance

### Owner check (after the web PR is merged and deployed to a test or the dev database with the seed)
1. As the General Manager, open Departments: the ten departments show with colors and icons in their order. Reorder two of them and save; the task board's department filter follows the new order.
2. Create "Video Production" with a description, a color and the film icon. Its page opens empty.
3. Add two members; open one member's notifications: "You joined Video Production as secondary". Make one "primary here": their old department still lists them as secondary.
4. Make one member the manager. Create a task for Video Production and assign it; the overview counts it. As that manager, see the workload table; as a plain employee, see only the counts.
5. Open "What it grants" on Internal Operations: the manager's permissions and system functions are listed; on Video Production: "no extra permissions".
6. Archive Design: the wizard lists its members, open tasks, services, template steps and projects; send everything to Video Production except services, sent to Marketing. Confirm. Design shows as archived; its open tasks are on Video Production with the same assignees; its services perform in Marketing; a closed Design task still shows "Design (archived)".
7. Archive Medical Consultation to Video Production; move a healthcare client's task to medical review: the General Manager receives the medical review request. Restore Medical Consultation: it returns empty, last in the order.
8. As the Operations manager: rename and recolor a department, manage members; "New department", "Archive" and "Restore" are not offered, and the API refuses them (403).
9. Open History on Design: creation is not there (seeded), but the archive with its counts, the edits and the membership changes are.

- **Architecture test**: the new routes declare access; `departments.manage` in the permission map.
- **E2E and RTL screenshots**: the departments list (normal, reorder, archived), the new-department dialog, the department page tabs as General Manager, department manager and employee, the archive wizard's two steps, an archived department page, and a department badge on the board and the task list.

## PR split (proposed)
1. **Departments as records** (db, contracts, api, web adjustments; no new screens): the migration of ADR 0043, `departmentCodeSchema` and `SYSTEM_DEPARTMENT_CODES`, the departments list read from the database wherever `DEPARTMENT_CODES` was used, the identity columns, the archived checks on writes, and the system-recipient fallback (rule 14). Behavior is unchanged while no department is archived.
2. **Departments API**: create, edit identity, order, archive preview and transfer, restore, members, overview, notices, audit, `departments.manage`, and their tests.
3. **Departments screens**: the list, the department page with its tabs, the dialogs and the wizard, `DepartmentBadge` across the app, permission labels, E2E and screenshots; `docs/ROADMAP.md` marks F23's first package done.
